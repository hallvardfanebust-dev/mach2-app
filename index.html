Mach 2

A times-table game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. The goal is to answer any problem in under two seconds. Support and contact.

Get in touch

Questions and bug reports are welcome at mach2.support@gmail.com. If you are reporting a problem, please mention your device and its iOS, iPadOS or macOS version. How we handle your email is described in the privacy policy.

Common questions

Why can I not find “Answer with your voice”?

It needs an iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later, and the device’s language set to Norwegian or English.

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes. The only exception is the first time you turn on “Answer with your voice”: your device may then need to download speech recognition for your language from Apple.
