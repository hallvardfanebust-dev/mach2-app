Privacy Policy

Mach 2 for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Last updated 6 November 2026

Norsk · Support

In short: Mach 2 has no user account and no sign-in. What the app stores stays on your device and is not sent to us. The app shows no ads, has no analytics and does no tracking. If you choose “Answer with your voice”, the app listens for your answer during a round, and your voice is processed on the device. If you write to us, or test the app through TestFlight, we receive some information about you. This is described in sections 7–9.

1. Who is responsible

Mach 2 is developed and published by Hallvard Fanebust, an independent developer in Norway. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Hallvard Fanebust is the controller for the processing described below.

Contact: mach2.support@gmail.com

2. Information stored on your device

Mach 2 stores the following only on your own device:

  • The players: the name the player types, the chosen plane, when the player was created, and settings for numerals and writing direction.
  • Each round: level, table size, whether it was practice, time, result, number of correct answers, average time, stars, lives used and runs of correct answers.
  • Each times-table fact: the answer times, the time limits and when the fact was last shown. This is used to choose which facts to practise more.
  • Trophies the player has earned, and when.
  • Which player was used last, and whether “Read the problem aloud” and “Answer with your voice” are on.

The name can be a nickname. The information lives in the app’s own storage and is deleted when you delete the app. The app does not sync with iCloud. The information may still be included in a backup you make to iCloud or to a computer. Such a backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

3. The microphone and “Answer with your voice”

“Answer with your voice” is a choice in the app’s settings. It is available on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and for now only in Norwegian and English.

  • Mach 2 listens for your answer when “Answer with your voice” is on. Your voice is processed on this device.
  • The microphone is used only during a round, from the countdown until the round ends or is paused.
  • The app does not record or store any sound. It uses only the number in your answer. The text from speech recognition is neither shown nor stored.
  • The first time the choice is turned on, your iPhone may need to download speech recognition for your language. The download comes from Apple and is handled by Apple.
  • The app asks for access to the microphone. You can withdraw access in your device’s Settings. “Answer with your voice” then turns off the next time the app shows the list of levels, and you answer with the keys.

“Read the problem aloud” uses the voices already on your device.

4. Purchases

Mach 2 may offer a one-time purchase, Expert. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your card details, your billing address or your Apple Account. Apple may process purchase information outside the EEA, including in the United States. Apple’s handling of the purchase is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

5. Children

Mach 2 can be used by both children and adults. The app collects no personal information, from children or from adults. A player’s name stays on the device and is not sent to us.

6. What we do not do

  • We have no servers and no database. The app has no user account.
  • We show no ads, and the app contains no advertising or tracking technology.
  • The app has no analytics or crash reporting service of its own, and we build no profile of anyone.
  • We do not sell, rent out or share any personal information.

7. What Apple may give us from the App Store

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us figures about the use of the app and crash reports. Apple states that this data is aggregated or otherwise does not identify you. If you have not chosen this, we receive no such data about your use. How Apple collects and handles the data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. We use it only to fix bugs and improve the app.

8. Testing through TestFlight

If you test Mach 2 through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple’s terms and privacy policy for TestFlight apply. According to Apple, crash reports and information about your use of the app are then shared with us, and testers cannot opt out of this. They may include your name and email address. Feedback you send yourself through TestFlight may also be linked to your name and email address. We use this information only to fix bugs and improve the app, and do not share it with anyone.

9. When you write to us

If you write to mach2.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and what you write. The email is held by Google (Gmail), which may process it outside the EEA, including in the United States. We use it, and the feedback from TestFlight, only to answer you and fix bugs, and delete it when the matter is closed. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and fixing bugs in the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

10. Retention

The information on your device is deleted when you delete the app. Email and feedback are deleted when the matter is closed, as described in section 9. Beyond that, we keep no personal information.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. This also applies to email you have sent us and to feedback from TestFlight. Write to the address above, and we will answer. Questions about a purchase should go to Apple. If you believe your information has been processed unlawfully, you can complain to the supervisory authority in your country. In Norway this is Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a significant way, the date at the top of the page is updated, and the change is described here.
